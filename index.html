What I do

I help companies design and build machine learning and LLM-based systems, from scoping and prototyping to deployment. My background is in mathematical statistics: PhD on survival analysis (LPSM, supervised by Olivier Lopez) and agrégation de mathématiques.

I currently work on:

  • LLM agents and retrieval systems: agentic workflows, hybrid search, human-in-the-loop design, evaluation, sovereign deployments with open-weight models.
  • Predictive modelling: forecasting, predictive maintenance, risk scoring, including messy real-world data.
  • Statistical modelling: survival and time-to-event analysis, churn, censored data, uncertainty quantification.

Experience

  • Independent consultant (2025–present). LLM-based agents for compliance workflows in the financial sector, with human-in-the-loop review and sovereign deployment.
  • Senior Data Scientist, C3 AI (2022–2024). Predictive maintenance for a global cement producer: per-asset models on 200+ assets with a custom MLOps layer, and a 90% reduction in false-positive alerts. Supply-chain risk forecasting, with a 40% reduction in MAE over the baseline.
  • Machine Learning Researcher, Zelros (2019–2022). AI for insurance: automated 40% of medical underwriting decisions, built a semantic search engine and a bandit-based recommender, and created CinnaMon (see below).

Research interests

Foundation models for tabular and time-to-event data, AI agents, evaluation of LLM agents, and quantitative finance (market microstructure, systematic strategies).
